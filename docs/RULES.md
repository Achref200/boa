# BOA — the non-negotiables

Each rule below caused a decision somewhere in this codebase. Breaking one is a
defect, even when the build is green.

---

## 1. The logo is the logo

**Rule.** The official BOA mark ships as-is. It is never redesigned, redrawn,
"simplified", vectorised by hand, distorted, stretched, re-coloured,
re-cropped, given a new wordmark, or regenerated by any image model.

**Why.** It is the one asset that already carries the brand's equity. An
AI-redrawn approximation is the single fastest way to make a real business look
fake to its own customers.

**In practice.**
- The asset lives in `public/brand/` and is referenced, never transformed.
- Clear space around it is protected by the header/footer layout, not by
  cropping the file.
- On dark ground it sits on `--color-ink`; there is no light-background variant
  unless BOA supplies one.
- If a placement seems to need a different mark, the placement is wrong.

---

## 2. Nothing about BOA is invented

**Rule.** No product, price, format, ingredient, INCI list, benefit, claim,
certification, award, partner, statistic, opening hour, address, phone number,
founder story or date appears in this repository unless BOA supplied it.

**Why.** Cosmetics claims are regulated. An invented "dermatologically tested"
line is a legal exposure, not a copywriting flourish. And a customer who catches
one invented fact stops believing the true ones.

**In practice.**
- `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md` is the register: column A is confirmed, column B is a
  professional assumption that is safe to change, column C is a placeholder.
- Placeholder copy is visibly a placeholder and is editable from `/admin`.
- A product with no description renders **no description section**.
- A missing image renders a labelled frame with the expected dimensions
  (`components/ui/MediaFrame.tsx`), not a stock photo.
- Company details BOA has not supplied are absent from the site and listed as
  outstanding on the admin dashboard.

---

## 3. No fabricated social proof

No reviews, testimonials, ratings, star counts, review totals, "as seen in"
logos, customer photos or follower counts unless they are real and BOA owns
them. The `reviews` table exists and is wired; it renders only rows an
administrator has actually approved. Zero approved reviews means the section
does not render — it does not render "no reviews yet" prominence either.

JSON-LD follows the same rule: no `aggregateRating` without real ratings.

---

## 4. The design must not read as a template

**Forbidden by default**

- The generic funnel: hero → three feature cards → stat row → testimonials → CTA.
- The "cosmetics template" palette: blush pink, sand beige, sage, cream gradients.
- Decorative gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, floating 3D shapes.
- Everything rounded (`rounded-2xl` on every surface), everything shadowed.
- Emoji as iconography.
- Centred everything, identical section rhythm down the whole page.

**Instead** — the decisions in `docs/BRAND.md`: a dark ground taken from the
logo's own field, a measured gold ramp, `--radius: 0`, editorial asymmetry, type
carrying the hierarchy, motion only where it explains a state change.

---

## 5. The backend is real

- No mock data arrays pretending to be tables.
- No `localStorage` or React state substituting for persistence.
- No admin screen whose "save" only updates the client.
- Every list, count and badge is a query.
- Seed data is clearly seed data, lives in `scripts/seed.ts`, and refuses to run
  when `NODE_ENV=production`.

---

## 6. The server decides

- The client sends **identifiers and quantities**. Never prices, never totals,
  never discount amounts, never stock levels.
- `modules/orders/pricing.ts` re-reads variant prices, publication state, stock,
  shipping zone and discount validity at the moment of quoting and again at the
  moment of ordering.
- Orders and reservations **snapshot** everything they display. A later rename or
  reprice cannot rewrite history — which is why products are soft-deleted and
  ordered variants are deactivated rather than removed.
- Availability shown in the UI is advisory. The database is the authority:
  conditional `UPDATE … WHERE stock >= n` for stock, a unique
  `(slot_id, seat_index)` row for a seat.

---

## 7. Payments and personal data

- **No raw card data.** Not in a form field, not in a log, not in a column, not
  in an error message. Card capture belongs to the gateway's own hosted flow.
- Payment providers sit behind `modules/payments/provider.ts`. Cash on delivery,
  cash on pickup and bank transfer are implemented; an online gateway is a new
  provider implementation plus configuration, not a rewrite.
- Webhooks are verified and deduplicated (`webhook_events` has a unique
  `(provider, external_id)`).
- Customer data is minimised: what an order needs to be delivered, nothing more.
- Secrets come from the environment through `src/lib/env.ts`, which is
  `server-only`. Nothing secret is ever committed.

---

## 8. Accessibility and quality are gates

- WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, ≥ 3:1 for large text and UI
  boundaries; visible focus; 44px touch targets; complete keyboard paths.
- The axe scan in `tests/e2e/accessibility.spec.ts` fails the build on a
  violation. It is not advisory.
- Every asynchronous surface has a loading, empty and error state.
- Every destructive admin action is confirmed and audited.
